Vision MOD Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Data Collection

Vision MOD does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only network activity is the App Store purchase and restore flow, which is handled by Apple.

Camera and Microphone

Vision MOD requests camera access to use the camera image as its visual source, and microphone or audio input access to use audio levels as modulation. Both are processed in real time on the device. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

AI Processing

All image generation runs locally on your Mac. No image is uploaded to any server.

Local Files

Settings, presets and any recordings you make are stored on your device and never leave it unless you export them yourself.

Third Parties

Vision MOD shares no data with third parties, because it collects none.
